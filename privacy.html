Privacy

Budget is a personal budgeting app used only by its owner. It has no server and no user accounts of its own.

  • Budget data is stored on the user's own devices and, if enabled, in a hidden app folder of the user's own Google Drive.
  • Bank transactions are retrieved through Enable Banking with the user's explicit consent, via a script running on the user's own Google account, and are only used to display them in the user's budget.
  • No data is sold, shared, analysed or sent to any third party.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the app (Disconnect bank) or from the bank.
